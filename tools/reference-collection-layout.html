Help

The active tool is blue and stays active after drawing a region. Drag to draw rectangles; click vertices to draw each polygon, then Finish polygon or press Enter. In Select / edit, drag from empty canvas to box-select polygons; Shift-drag anywhere adds to the selection. Left-drag inside a selected polygon to move all selected polygons together; Ctrl/Cmd-click toggles individual regions. Click an edge to select it for 1-pixel nudges, Add vertex, or Mirror boundary; dragging an edge adjusts only that edge. Rectangles keep their shape: drag a corner or side to resize it. Drag a polygon vertex to reshape it. Convert to rectangle replaces a polygon with its bounding rectangle; Convert to polygon keeps a rectangle's four corners and unlocks polygon editing. Algorithm proposals must be adopted before editing.

Ctrl/Cmd+C copies the selected vertex, edge, or region(s). Ctrl/Cmd+V adds a vertex, mirrors the copied edge onto a selected neighboring region or facing edge, or clones copied regions in the first direction that fits: below, right, above, then left. Ctrl/Cmd+X and Delete remove the selected vertex, edge, or region. For an edge, the right-hand endpoint remains; convert rectangles to polygons before deleting an edge.
